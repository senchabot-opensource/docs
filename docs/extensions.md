# Extensions

[Senchabot Extensions](https://extensions.senchabot.com) are free widgets and tools for your stream. Each one is set up on its own page and added to OBS as a browser source — most need no login, and many are controlled from Twitch or Kick chat.

## Overlays

- **[Chat Box](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/chat-widget)** — Twitch, Kick and YouTube chat together in one overlay, with 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes.
- **[Event List](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/event-list)** — Your latest subs, gifted subs, Bits, Kicks and raids from both chats in one list.
- **[Emote Wall](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/emote-wall)** — Emote-only chat messages float across your screen.
- **[Emote Combo](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/emote-combo)** — When chat spams the same emote, a big combo counter shows how many joined in.
- **[Sub Sprout](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/sub-growing-plant)** — A plant on your stream that grows a little with every new sub.
- **[Follower Goal](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/follower-goal)** — A goal bar that every new follow fills in real time, with a trophy when you reach it. Open its setup page from the [Senchabot Dashboard](https://senchabot.com).
- **[Bits & Kicks Goal](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/bits-goal)** — A goal bar that every Bits cheer and Kicks gift fills, with a trophy at the end.
- **[Top Supporters](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/top-supporters)** — Your top gifters and cheerers from Twitch and Kick, or your latest sub, gift, cheer and raid.
- **[Stream Alerts](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/stream-alerts)** — An animated alert with a sound for every sub, gifted sub, Bits, Kicks and raid, and for every follow with Senchabot.
- **[End Credits](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/end-credits)** — Movie-style credits for everyone who subbed, cheered, raided and chatted on Twitch and Kick.
- **[Socials](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/socials)** — Rotate your social media links with a slick animation.
- **[Sub Goal](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/sub-goal)** — A goal bar that every sub and gifted sub fills, with a trophy when you reach it.
- **[Now Playing](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/now-playing)** — The song you're listening to, with its cover art, read from Last.fm.
- **[Stream Frames](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/stream-frames)** — Ready-made frames for your camera, chat and stream screen, drawn to match your preset.
- **[Lower Thirds](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/lower-thirds)** — An animated name and topic bar for a corner of your stream, with lines that take turns.
- **[IRL HUD Overlay](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/irl-hud)** — A game-style HUD for IRL streams: subs, gifts, cheers and raids add cash, with quests and a mini-map.
- **[Weather](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/weather)** — The live weather on your IRL stream for any place you pick, with an icon, the temperature and a forecast.

## Tools

- **[Chat Poll](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/chat-poll)** — A poll your chat votes in by typing a number, with live bars and a winner.
- **[Viewer Queue](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/viewer-queue)** — Viewers type !join to get in line to play with you, and your mods call them up with !next.
- **[Featured Message](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/featured-message)** — Put a viewer's message or question on screen, from Twitch or Kick chat.
- **[Raffle](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/raffle)** — Viewers join with a chat keyword like !join, and you draw the winner.
- **[Subathon Timer](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/subathon-timer)** — A countdown that subs, gifted subs, Bits and Kicks push back. Health bar, clock or ring.
- **[Counter](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/counter)** — Wins, losses or any count on your stream. Your mods change it from chat.
- **[Goal List](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/goal-list)** — Sub, Bits, Kicks and follower goals your mods tick off from chat.
- **[OBS Bridge](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/obs-bridge)** — Switch OBS scenes, show, hide and mute sources, and control your IRL stream from chat. Nothing to download, no login.
- **[Spin Wheel](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/spin-wheel)** — Viewers spin the wheel from chat with !spin. No login needed.
- **[Task List](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/task-list)** — A co-working task list your chat fills with !task and ticks off with !done.
- **[Stream Countdown](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/stream-countdown)** — A countdown for your starting, break and ending scenes, set by length or by clock time.
- **[Clip Replay](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/clip-replay)** — Your best clips on a loop: chat votes for the next one out of three. Kick clips need no login; Twitch clips need a Senchabot login from the Dashboard.
