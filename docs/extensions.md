# Extensions

[Senchabot Extensions](https://extensions.senchabot.com) are free widgets and tools for your stream. Each one is set up on its own page and added to OBS as a browser source — no login needed, and most of them are controlled from Twitch or Kick chat.

## Overlays

- **[Chat Box](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/chat-widget)** — Twitch and Kick chat together in one overlay, with 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes.
- **[Emote Wall](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/emote-wall)** — Emote-only chat messages float across your screen.
- **[Sub Sprout](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/sub-growing-plant)** — A plant on your stream that grows a little with every new sub.
- **[Stream Alerts](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/stream-alerts)** — An animated alert with a sound for every sub, gifted sub, Bits, Kicks and raid.
- **[Sub Goal](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/sub-goal)** — A goal bar that every sub and gifted sub fills, with a trophy when you reach it.
- **[Stream Frames](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/stream-frames)** — Ready-made frames for your camera, chat and stream screen, drawn to match your preset.
- **[Stream Countdown](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/stream-countdown)** — A countdown for your starting, break and ending scenes, set by length or by clock time.
- **[Socials](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/socials)** — Rotate your social media links with a slick animation.

## Tools

- **[Chat Poll](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/chat-poll)** — A poll your chat votes in by typing a number, with live bars and a winner.
- **[Raffle](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/raffle)** — Viewers join with a chat keyword like !join, and you draw the winner.
- **[Subathon Timer](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/subathon-timer)** — A countdown that subs, gifted subs, Bits and Kicks push back. Health bar, clock or ring.
- **[OBS Bridge](https://extensions.senchabot.com/setup/obs-bridge)** — Switch OBS scenes and control streaming and recording from chat commands.
