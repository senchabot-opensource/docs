# Kick Bot — Stream Manager <Badge type="warning" text="NEW"/>

One tab to run your whole stream: live chat, title and category, activity, TTS, and moderation. Open **Dashboard → Stream Manager** (`/dashboard/stream-manager`). Multistreaming Kick + Twitch? Everything below works across both — no tab-switching.

## Retitle mid-stream
Change your title or category without stopping: target **Kick, Twitch, or Both**, type a new title, search categories with autocomplete (*Just Chatting*, *Valorant*), manage Kick tags. If title or category drifts between platforms, copy title, category, or tags from either side — or push one side's title/category to both — in one click.

## One chat for both platforms
Kick and Twitch messages side by side with platform and chatter badges. Send to either chat, reply inline, toggle 7TV emotes, filter by @user, clear the local view, or pop the chat out for a second monitor or OBS dock. Scroll up and auto-scroll pauses with a **New messages** pill, so you never lose your place.

## Moderate from any message
Delete, timeout, ban, or unban right from the message card, with `(deleted by @user)` attribution. Missing Kick scopes? A banner grants them in one click.

## TTS that keeps up
Browser-powered voice for chat: fixed language or auto-detected per message, optional name and platform announcements (*"On Twitch, ..."*), a cleaner that strips URLs, spam repeats, and emote floods, a one-button emergency stop — and anything deleted or banned mid-queue is yanked from the speech queue instantly.

## Activity feed
Follows, subs and gift subs, raids with viewer counts, Kicks (`@{user} gifted {kicks} Kicks`) — plus timeout/ban and bot-command audit logs. Optional chimes, read-aloud activities, and a popout window.

## Stats at a glance
Live/offline indicator, uptime counter, session chatters and message counts, recent-stream history, collapsible platform panels.

## Give your mods access
No password sharing: **Dashboard → Kick → `<channel>` → Settings → Moderators**, search a username, **Add**, revoke anytime. Your channel lands under **Channels I Moderate** in their switcher, and in Stream Manager they pick you from **Select Streamer** to moderate, track, and retitle on your behalf.
